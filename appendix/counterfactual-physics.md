# Counterfactual Physics — If One Axiom Were Different

> **L1 from ROADMAP**: Systematic counterfactual analysis of R1-R6.  
> **Motivation**: The strongest evidence for the necessity of each axiom is answering: "If this axiom were different, what would break?"

---

## R1: Causal Spacetime Structure

### If D ≠ 3+1 (spatial dimensionality change)

| Dimensionality | Affected Laws | Concrete Consequences |
|---|---|---|
| D=2 space | Inverse-square → 1/r | Gauss flux ∝ r (circumference), not r² (area). Gravity too weak—no stable planetary orbits. Centrifugal potential ∝ 1/r² overwhelms gravitational potential ∝ ln r at small r. |
| D=2 space | No connected organisms | In 2D, connected objects cannot have through-holes (no simultaneous mouth and anus). Complex biology topologically impossible. |
| D=4 space | Inverse-square → 1/r³ | Gravity too strong—no stable orbits. Electrons spiral into nucleus instantly or escape. No chemistry, no life. |
| D even | Huygens' principle fails | Wave propagation has "afterglow"—signal does not return to silence after passing. Every conversation overlaps with echoes of previous ones. |
| D=5 | SO(5) rotation group | 10 angular momentum generators (SO(5) dim = 5×4/2 = 10). Quantum angular momentum structure fundamentally different. |
| D=5 | GW polarization modes | 5 polarization modes (D(D-3)/2 = 5) instead of 2. Gravitational wave detection would look entirely different. |

**Key reference**: Ehrenfest (1917), "In what way does it become manifest in the fundamental laws of physics that space has three dimensions?" *Proc. Amsterdam Acad.*, 20, 200-209.

### If Lorentz signature → Euclidean signature

| Change | Consequence |
|---|---|
| (+,-,-,-) → (+,+,+,+) | No causal order—all directions equivalent. No "past" vs "future". |
| Euclidean signature | No wave propagation—wave equation □φ=0 becomes Laplace equation ∇²φ=0. No traveling wave solutions. |
| Euclidean signature | QFT cannot define spectral condition—no distinction between positive and negative frequency. No particle concept. |
| Euclidean signature | No E=mc²—mass-energy equivalence lost. |

### If no speed-of-light limit (c→∞, Galilei invariance)

| Change | Consequence |
|---|---|
| c→∞ | Causal paradoxes—closed timelike curves possible (time travel). |
| c→∞ | Lorentz → Galileo transform—absolute time and space restored. |
| c→∞ | No E=mc²—no nuclear energy, no stellar fusion. |
| c→∞ | No electromagnetic waves—Maxwell's c=1/√(ε₀μ₀) would be infinite, EM fields don't propagate. |
| c→∞ | No stable atoms—electrons can orbit at arbitrary speeds. |

### If equivalence principle fails (mᵢ ≠ m_g)

| Change | Consequence |
|---|---|
| mᵢ ≠ m_g | Gravity cannot be geometrized—g_μν cannot be a dynamical field. |
| mᵢ ≠ m_g | Einstein field equations cannot be derived—GR does not exist. |
| mᵢ ≠ m_g | Gravity remains a "force" (Newtonian), not spacetime geometry—strong equivalence principle fails. |
| mᵢ ≠ m_g | Gravitational waves still exist (as field waves) but not as "ripples of spacetime metric". |
| mᵢ/m_g varies by composition | Different materials fall at different rates—no universal gravitational field. |

**Experimental bound**: |mᵢ/m_g - 1| < 10⁻¹⁵ (MICROSCOPE satellite, 2022). This is the most precise non-trivial null result in physics.

| Change | Consequence |
|---|---|
| No δS=0 | No Euler-Lagrange equations—all equations of motion lose unified derivation framework. |
| No δS=0 | No Noether's theorem—symmetry↔conservation connection severed. |
| No δS=0 | Newton's laws, Maxwell's equations, Einstein field equations must each be independently postulated. Unity of physics lost. |

## R3: Local Gauge Invariance

### If no gauge symmetry

| Change | Consequence |
|---|---|
| No local gauge symmetry | No gauge fields—no fundamental interaction forces (photon, W/Z, gluons do not exist). |
| Only global U(1) | Charge conservation exists but no Maxwell equations—electric charge without electromagnetic field. |
| Non-Abelian → Abelian | Gauge bosons have no self-coupling—QCD has no asymptotic freedom—quarks form free particles. |
| SU(3)×SU(2)×U(1) → other gauge group | Different particle spectrum and forces—"Standard Model" completely different. |

### If the gauge group of our universe were different

| Gauge Group | Consequence |
|---|---|
| No SU(3) color | No strong force. No protons or neutrons—quarks are free. No atomic nuclei. |
| No SU(2) weak isospin | No W±, Z bosons. No weak interactions. No beta decay. Stars burn differently. |
| No U(1) hypercharge | No photon after SSB. No electromagnetism. |
| Different number of colors (Nc ≠ 3) | QCD confinement scale shifts. Nc=3 is the minimum for baryons (qqq) to be color singlets. |

## R4: Quantum Postulates

### If [x̂,p̂] = 0 (commuting position and momentum)

| Change | Consequence |
|---|---|
| [x̂,p̂] = 0 | No uncertainty principle—position and momentum simultaneously precisely determinable. No zero-point energy (ℏω/2). |
| [x̂,p̂] = 0 | No discrete energy spectrum—atoms unstable (electrons spiral into nucleus). No periodic table. |
| [x̂,p̂] = 0 | No tunneling—α decay impossible. Nuclear physics fundamentally different. |
| [x̂,p̂] = 0 | Spin-statistics theorem fails—no Pauli exclusion. All particles can occupy same state. Matter collapses. |

**Key insight**: [x̂,p̂]=iℏ is not "an optional assumption of quantum mechanics"—it is a **necessary condition for stable matter to exist**. Without the non-commutativity, Noether's theorem gives angular momentum and energy, but no discreteness—atoms would spiral into the nucleus. iℏ is not optional—it is required for matter to exist.

### If unitary evolution → non-unitary

| Change | Consequence |
|---|---|
| Non-unitary evolution | Probability not conserved—particles can appear or vanish. QM loses predictive power. |
| Linear → nonlinear Schrödinger | Superluminal signaling possible (Gisin 1990). Violates causal structure (R1). |

### If classical mechanics (ℏ=0)

| Change | Consequence |
|---|---|
| ℏ=0 | No quantum transitions, no superconductivity, no lasers, no semiconductors. All quantum technologies nonexistent. |
| ℏ=0 | Atoms unstable (electromagnetic radiation from accelerating electrons → spiral into nucleus in ~10⁻¹¹ s). No chemistry. |

## R5: Born Rule

### If P ≠ |ψ|²

| Change | Consequence |
|---|---|
| P ≠ |ψ|² | QM loses connection to experiment—theory self-consistent but untestable. |
| P = |ψ|⁴ | Interference pattern not cos-type—double-slit fringe spacing different from observation. |
| Nonlinear Born rule | Superluminal signals possible (Gisin 1990 theorem)—violates causal structure (R1). |
| No Born rule | R4's Hilbert space formalism becomes pure mathematics—no physical predictive power. |

**Key insight (Gleason 1957)**: In Hilbert spaces of dimension ≥ 3, the Born rule P = Tr(ρE) is the **unique** probability measure satisfying additivity. It is not "chosen"—it is the only mathematical possibility compatible with Hilbert space + probability axioms.

### If the Born rule assigned amplitudes differently

| p-value in P = |⟨a|ψ⟩|^p | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| p=1 (modulus, not squared) | Normalization ∫|ψ|dx=1 fails for general ψ. Superposition principle breaks. |
| p=4 (fourth power) | Interference not simple cos-type. Double-slit experiment produces **different fringe spacing**—but experiment shows cos-type. |
| p arbitrary ≠ 2 | Gleason's theorem excludes all p≠2 in dim H ≥ 3. Experimental bound on deviation: < 10⁻¹⁰. |

## R6: Statistical Mechanics Foundation

### If no S = k_B ln W

| Change | Consequence |
|---|---|
| No S = k_B ln W | No micro-macro bridge—thermodynamics remains empirical phenomenology, not derivable from microphysics. |
| No S = k_B ln W | Temperature becomes empirical scale—no 1/T = ∂S/∂E definition. |
| No S = k_B ln W | Quantum statistics (FD, BE) cannot be derived—only empirical fits. |

### If no equal prior probability postulate

| Change | Consequence |
|---|---|
| P_i arbitrary | All statistical mechanics predictions (canonical, grand canonical, fluctuation-dissipation) lose foundation. |
| P_i arbitrary | Second law of thermodynamics stops being a theorem—becomes an empirical observation with no microscopic derivation. |
| P_i arbitrary | Statistical mechanics reduces to pure combinatorics (W can be counted but has no probabilistic weight). |

**Key insight**: The equal prior probability postulate is the **only assumption in statistical mechanics that cannot be derived from R1-R5**. It is an independent postulate. Its justification comes from ergodicity: for sufficiently complex many-body systems, dynamics "visits" all microstates with equal frequency over long timescales.

## Cross-Axiom Counterfactuals

### If the world were Newton's (c→∞ + ℏ=0 + no gauge symmetry)

The "classical world" of the late 19th century:
- Spacetime is absolute—Galileo transform, no Lorentz transform.
- Mechanics is deterministic—no probability, no quantum states.
- Forces: only Newton gravity + Coulomb electrostatics—no Maxwell EM waves, no weak/strong forces.
- Atoms don't exist (electrons spiral into nucleus). No chemistry, no life.

**Newton's world cannot support our familiar universe.** This is not "Newton's mechanics is wrong"—it is extraordinarily accurate in daily life—but its axiomatic foundation is insufficient to derive the full physical structure of the real world.

### If quantum but no causal structure (ℏ>0 + c→∞)

- Schrödinger equation exists (non-relativistic QM), but wavefunctions can propagate instantaneously.
- No spin-statistics theorem (requires Lorentz invariance).
- No Pauli exclusion—matter collapses.
- No quantum field theory—cannot describe particle creation/annihilation.

### If D=3+1 + ℏ>0 + c<∞ but no equivalence principle

- Special relativity + QM work ("flat-spacetime QFT").
- Maxwell's equations work.
- But no GR—gravity remains Newtonian.
- No gravitational waves, no black holes, no cosmic expansion.
- At Planck scale, Newtonian gravity + QFT give infinities—theory is inconsistent.

## Necessity vs. Contingency

The counterfactual analysis reveals a fundamental distinction:

**Necessary** (mathematically forced given R1-R6):
- Lorentz transformation (from light-speed invariance + spacetime homogeneity)
- Maxwell's equations (from U(1) gauge + least action)
- Einstein field equations (from Lovelock: unique 2nd-order metric Lagrangian in 4D)
- Schrödinger equation (from Stone's theorem + unitary evolution)
- Born rule (from Gleason: unique probability measure in Hilbert space)
- Pauli exclusion (from spin-statistics theorem)

**Contingent** (logically possible but this universe happens to be so):
- Spacetime dimensionality D=3+1
- Gauge group SU(3)×SU(2)×U(1)
- Fermion content (3 generations of quarks + leptons)
- Specific values of free parameters (Yukawa couplings, CKM phases, etc.)

This distinction is one of the core contributions of physics axiomatization: **it tells us which features of our universe are "forced" and which are "chosen"**.
